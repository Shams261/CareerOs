# Changelog

## Unreleased

- WI-046 (one truth for problems, P0 from the UAT audit, ADR-025): a roadmap item linked to a Practice problem now follows Practice. No attempt reads Not started, any attempt In progress, Green Done, and a later Yellow or Red reopens it. Its revision is the problem's, so a problem never shows two revision dates or appears twice in Revise. Recording an attempt, linking a problem, restoring an item and the import keep the item in step in the same transaction; Practice is never written by the roadmap. On the roadmap, a problem item's Done, Start, Reopen and Revised give way to **Record an attempt** (the row's quick button reads Attempt), and Edit hides "Revise on". The problem page gains a **Study** section with the roadmap item's Copy prompt. ⌘K lists a problem once. No migration.
- WI-045 (fresh start, P0 from the UAT audit): `scripts/fresh-start.sql`, an owner-run reset for a production workspace that holds only test data but already has the imported curriculum. In one transaction it deletes everything the owner did (routines, plans, blocks, sessions, reviews, notes, inbox, applications with rounds and prep docs, attempts, revision cards, Ask Rumi conversations and lines, noticed answers) and every test-named record (the `isTestNamed` rule in SQL, checked against the TypeScript rule), resets the remaining roadmap items, practice problems and learning topics to zero progress, and keeps sign-in, devices, settings, the AI cost ledger and the Google Calendar connection. It refuses unless there is exactly one user and is safe to run twice. Runbook in `docs/engineering/backup-restore.md`. No app change, no migration.
- WI-044 (security, P0 from the UAT audit of 2026-10-27): Next.js 16.3.6 carried two critical advisories (unauthenticated remote code execution in Image Optimization and `use cache`) and several high ones, and `/_next/image` answers without sign-in. Upgraded Next.js and `eslint-config-next` to 16.4.3, zod to 4.6.7, Prisma to 7.10.1 and vitest to 5.0.5, with `pnpm-workspace.yaml` overrides for `source-map-js`, `fast-uri` and `vite`; `pnpm audit` now reports no known vulnerabilities. CI fails on a high or critical production advisory (`pnpm audit --prod --audit-level high`), and Dependabot opens weekly update pull requests. No product change, no migration.
- WI-043 (Rumi noticed, the last item of the Rumi plan): Needs attention on Today can start with up to two **Rumi noticed** rows a day, found by code from the owner's own records and written from fixed templates, with no AI. The four rules: an interview in the next three days with prep items open ("Fabrikam Coding in 2 days, 1 prep item open" → Open prep); a study kind silent for five days or more that the owner planned or recorded in four weeks ("No Backend recorded in 9 days" → Plan it with Rumi, which hands the question to Ask Rumi); a routine left on every occurrence for three weeks (→ Open routines); and today or tomorrow planned at twice the owner's usual recorded study or more ("Tomorrow plans 6h of study; you usually record about 2h" → Open Plan). Each row has its action and Dismiss. An action hides the row for 3 days and Dismiss for 7, and both are counted (`NoticedAnswer`) to measure usefulness. Study kinds are the owner's own (Study nudges setting); test-named records never count; no template uses a banned word. Migration `20261027180000_rumi_noticed`.
- WI-042 (interview prep doc, Rumi plan, ADR-024): an open application offers **Prepare with Rumi**. The panel first shows the searches (only the company, the role and round types reach Exa), what the writing call gets (research, the rounds, roadmap item and problem titles; never notes, contacts, compensation or reflections), an optional tick for the job description, and the cost (about US$0.06). **Research and write** runs up to six web searches through the `web` plugin with Exa on the pinned zero-retention endpoint, then writes a doc: the process, what each round tests, topics linked to the owner's own roadmap items and problems, a seven-day plan with **Plan this with Rumi**, and questions to ask. Every point cites its sources; points without a real source and links to items that do not exist are dropped, and a thin doc is not shown. Sources list their site and date and are marked **Official** (the company's own domain or the posting's host) or **Other**. Five docs a day, inside the shared US$3 month. Docs are deletable and in the export, and Rumi can read the latest ones (`get_prep_docs`). New table `InterviewPrepDoc` and ledger feature `PREP_RESEARCH` (migration `20261027150000_prep_docs`).
- WI-041 (Rumi plans, Rumi plan, ADR-024): Rumi can now **propose a plan**. Asked "kal ka plan bana do", it reads the day and the owner's patterns and shows a card for one day from today to this Sunday: each block with its time, kind and a reason from the records ("Technical: 0 min in the last 7 days"). A block that does not fit says why ("Won't be added: Overlaps 18:00–19:00 busy") and is never written. **Add to plan** re-checks every block against the day under the owner lock (routines for that day generated first) and adds them all or none as blocks added for the day, synced to Google Calendar, with Undo, which cancels them and reopens the card. Dismiss closes it. Next week stays with Prepare next week. Rumi can also suggest a **Rumi knows** line, kept only when the owner presses Keep (duplicates are skipped). A new read tool, `get_patterns`, gives the three-hour window with the most recorded study and the recorded share of planned time per kind over four weeks, and Weekly review shows the same as one line under the week chart. Cards live in the answer's metadata, so there is no migration; proposals shown, applied and dismissed stay countable there.
