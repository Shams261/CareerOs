# WI-014 handoff — design review rows 2–10

Branch `feat/wi-014-design-review-batch` from `main` (after PRs #18 and #19). Scope: the nine items the owner listed from `docs/design-review/ENHANCEMENTS.md` section F, rows 2–10, in the order given. Six commits, one or two rows each. No schema change, no migration, no new dependency.

## 1. Outcome by item

1. **Done keeps its confirmation and Next; Revised only when due; Reopen undo.** The open filter keeps items completed today (owner-local day), so Done leaves the row with its message and "Next:" link. The row quick action and the panel offer Revised only when the revision is due or overdue; a done item that is not due shows no primary in its panel. Reopen returns an undo payload (`restore-roadmap-state`: status, completedAt, nextRevisionAt, revisionStage) and says "Reopened; the revision schedule is cleared." Start and unasked got their own messages.
2. **Practice.** `PatternGroups` is keyed by the search and status, so filters open the bands that match. When nothing is current or due the first pattern opens. The band markup dropped the table/row/cell roles (axe `aria-required-children` critical ×17 is gone). The attempt form starts with no answer and no confidence (Save is blocked until the owner answers). "Complexity" became "Difficulty".
3. **Reachable undo.** New `src/components/feedback-channel.ts` (publish/subscribe) and `feedback-outlet.tsx`. `ActionForm` takes `broadcast="<channel>"`: the result is published inside the action wrapper, before React commits the refreshed page, and the form itself skips its own success message. Outlets sit outside the rows: Today's aside (`notes`), `PriorityList` (`priorities`), the roadmap `ItemList` (`roadmap-items`), the application's Next action card (`next-action`) and each round (`round-<id>`). `stageAction` returns `restore-stage` undo (moves the application back; history stays append-only with one more entry). `DisclosureTabs` remembers the open tab by label and closes when that label disappears. Make current stays mounted (disabled when current). Learn's reminder block became a link to Settings.
4. **Today hierarchy.** `.hero .button:not(.secondary)` is white on teal. `ExecutionControls` makes Start primary only for the current or running block, offers Stop and Complete only while running, and the Now card (today only) carries the current block's controls with the `S` shortcut; timeline Starts are secondary and allowed for today only. "Save daily review" and "Review week" are secondary.
5. **Phone shell and asides.** `Navigation` takes a `capture` node rendered in the More sheet; the sidebar's note field is hidden under 700 px (header 245 → 55 px at 390). The bar is Today · Roadmap · Practice · Jobs · More; Plan, Weekly review and Settings sit behind More. Under 1050 px the Today aside is a flex column with the daily review first (`.daily-review`). The application page's Stage card is gone; "Change stage" is a disclosure in the summary line (now a `div`).
6. **Row recipe.** Open roadmap titles wrap; under 700 px rows are icon · title · (meta on line two), the hover quick action is hidden on touch and narrow screens, and the search and status controls share one row. `pointer: coarse` gives small buttons, link buttons, pill links, item titles, disclosure links and sheet links a 44 px floor; the external-link icon has a 24 px target. The Practice grid lost the Pattern column (`1fr 110px 200px`) and collapses at 1100 px.
7. **Settings and reminders.** Daily reminder defaults to off until saved. One `findMany` loads the four preferences. Job reminders moved into Settings under the daily and revision reminders; all three use `.dsa-form` with secondary Save buttons and "Time" labels; Jobs and Learn link to Settings. Theme and calendar connect are secondary. Push errors distinguish a timeout; "unsupported" names browsers that work.
8. **Jobs forms and vocabulary.** Quick add shows company and role; URL, source, applied date and stage sit behind More details. Round form: type first, title optional (server defaults it to the type label; `roundInput.title` optional), "Add to my schedule" on by default (`roundAction` calls `addInterviewToSchedule`), the rest behind More details. `ownerLabel` is "Your move" / "Waiting on company" / "No one yet" everywhere; "Whose move" on the form; overdue text in sentence case; stage and details saves secondary.
9. **Weekly review and Plan.** `reviewInput.intent` gains `next`: "Save and continue →" saves a draft then `redirect`s to step three (text verified kept). `completeReview` (service) and `CompleteReviewForm` close the review from step three; "Complete review" left step two. A past week's step three explains that Prepare is for the coming week and links to this week. Routine edit replies "Routine saved. Days already planned keep their times." when nothing planned changed; options read "Only days not planned yet" / "Also this week's planned days (preview first)"; Remove routine is a `button destructive`. `DateJump` ignores years under 2000 and is keyed by its value on Today and Plan. Plan shows the Prepare form when the viewed week is unplanned. "day(s)" is pluralised.

## 4. Risks and follow-ups

- The feedback channel is in-page state: a message published while the outlet is not mounted (it always is, on the pages wired here) is dropped silently. Wire new lists the same way: `broadcast` on the row form, one `FeedbackOutlet` outside the rows.
- "Add to my schedule" creates a `TimeBlock` for every round scheduled with the box checked; untick it for rounds that should not appear on Today.
- The Monday review path and the `pointer: coarse` sizes need a real phone to confirm (the pane emulates size, not pointer type).
- Left from the review: rows 11–15 (copy and formats, accessibility pass, buttons and tokens, transitions, error and not-found pages).
