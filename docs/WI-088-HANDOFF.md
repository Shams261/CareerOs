# WI-088 handoff — the roadmap as weeks

Date: 2026-10-30. Branch `feat/wi-088-roadmap-weeks` from `main` `4fcb7bf` (WI-087).

**Source.** The owner rejected the outline and reading pane (WI-084, WI-085, WI-086) after using them and asked for a complete revamp in their own words: the goal centred and bold on the Roadmap page, no automatic picks there, the default roadmaps as the page, a button to add their own, roadmaps that open like Practice's Arrays & Hashing band (Backend engineering → Week 1 … Week 8), and a week as its own page with its items as dropdown rows. The design was drawn first (`output/Silsila-Roadmap-Revamp-Mock.pdf`, not committed: screens in Silsila's tokens, eight suggestions with Yes / No, the long-roadmap choice) and approved with every suggestion and option (a), all sections listed.

**Migration:** none. Nothing new is stored except the open band in the browser (`localStorage` `silsila-roadmap-open`).
