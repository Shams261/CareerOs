# Silsila whole-app UI/UX design review — report

Date 2026-10-21 · branch `feat/wi-012-roadmap-tracker` at `865de71` · read-only: nothing outside `docs/design-review/` was changed.

Companion files: [ia.md](ia.md) (experience map, task paths, design foundation), [ENHANCEMENTS.md](ENHANCEMENTS.md) (what to do, in order), the three detailed findings files ([Today/Plan/Review](findings-today-plan-review.md), [Jobs/Settings/Login/Learn](findings-jobs-settings-login-learn.md), [Roadmap/Practice/Problem](findings-roadmap-practice-problem.md)) and `screenshots/` (49 captures at 390, 768 and 1440 px, light and dark).

## Method and substitutions

- **Walkthrough:** every screen and state in the desktop app's built-in browser, signed in as the fake owner (`pnpm google:fake` + `pnpm dev`, `silsila_dev_empty` with the imported roadmaps and the example seed). Viewports 1440×900, 768×1024 and 390×844, plus dark mode via the `silsila-theme` cookie. The browser pane is 800×500, so 1440 captures are rendered at that size; phone and tablet captures are full size.
- **Playwright was not used.** It is not a dependency of this repository and the brief said not to add anything. The built-in browser gave the same screenshots and let axe-core run. Every claim marked "verified" below was seen in the browser; claims from code reading say so.
- **Automated accessibility:** axe-core 4.13 (the copy already in `node_modules`) run on each page with the `wcag2a`, `wcag2aa`, `wcag22aa` and `best-practice` tags. It was served from a temporary file under `public/` that has been removed.
- **Code review:** three read-only passes over the screens' components, services and CSS (one per screen group). Their full tables, with `file:line` evidence, are the findings files. This report merges them with the browser evidence and keeps the rows that matter most.
- **Not covered:** real Web Push, Google Calendar connect, real devices and screen readers (VoiceOver not run), the export file, and the one-time production reset runbook.

## Executive summary

1. The product's bones are right: one owner, plain labels, server-rendered pages, in-place forms, undo on most writes, a calm token set that survives dark mode, visible focus rings and a skip link. Nothing needs a redesign.
2. The two daily loops break at their last step. On the roadmap page, marking a long imported section's item **Done** removes the row together with its confirmation and "Next" link (P1), and from Today's "Revise today" link you land on a different item than the one you meant to revise (P0).
3. **Undo is wired but unreachable** in four places (notes, review priorities, roadmap Remove, job stage changes) because the form that shows the Undo button is the row that disappears.
4. **The Monday weekly review targets the wrong week** by default (P0), and with eight or fewer applications **offers and closed applications vanish from Jobs** (P0).
5. On the phone, the shared header (logo, note field, Save) spends 29% of the first screen, roadmap titles truncate to a few letters, and several controls are 32–36 px.
6. Practice's pattern bands are a good shape, but search leaves the matches inside closed bands (verified), the ARIA table markup is invalid (axe critical, 17 nodes), and after an import nothing is open and nothing is "next".
7. Hierarchy slips: five to seven filled primary buttons on Today, Settings and the application page; the running session's Stop button is teal on teal.
8. Consistency debt is small but everywhere: raw `<button>` in 23 files against the shared `Button` in 7; 36 unicode glyphs beside lucide icons; ISO dates next to "Oct 28"; one serif heading; 612 px literals and two uses of the spacing tokens; no page titles.
9. Of 178 detailed findings, 3 are P0, 19 are P1, and roughly 85% are size S. Two weeks of small changes clear everything above P2.
10. Recommended order: fix the three P0s and the Done/undo loop first (two days), then the phone shell and the primary-button rule, then the consistency batches already planned in `docs/design/consistency-plan.md`.
