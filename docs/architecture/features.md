# Features

What each part of Silsila does and where it lives. Each entry names the work item that built it and the later ones that changed it; the full history is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md), and older handoffs are listed in [HISTORY](../HISTORY.md). The rules every feature keeps are in `CLAUDE.md` (core invariants, time rules, security, brand and UI rules) and the [decision records](decisions.md).

## Daily loop

- **Schedule and execution (WI-001/002):** weekly RoutineBlocks, idempotent DailyPlan/TimeBlock generation, per-occurrence overrides, start/stop/resume/complete/skip/cancel, manual sessions, one running session per owner, daily check-in.
- **Today as an agenda (WI-020, ADR-019):** `src/app/today/page.tsx` composes `src/features/today/` (pure view models in `domain.ts`: `daySummary`, `launcherState`, `agendaEntries`, `groupAgenda`, `blockResolution`, `attentionItems`). One factual summary line, one launcher with one primary action, the agenda as an open list, one bounded Needs attention list (five rows, a paged panel), and side panels for the review, the chain, notes, block details and the full queue. A linked interview and its block are one row through `TimeBlock.interviewRoundId`; reminders merge into attention rows by the entity in their `dedupeKey` and keep their ids. No ring, no score, no streak; past blocks without a decision read "Not recorded" or "Recorded · Needs update", never missed. Adding something to Today means a row in Needs attention or a new decision.
- **Plan as days (WI-021, ADR-020; one list since WI-083):** `src/app/calendar/page.tsx` renders the week as one list of the shared block rows with a line per day (`src/features/schedule/row.ts` for `blockResolution`, `resolutionText`, `blockTitle`, `rowMeta`, `blockFacts`; `src/features/schedule/block-row.tsx` for the `li.agenda-row` shell), a strip of day chips and one facts line; routines live in a side panel; acting on a block happens on Today. Times are 24-hour everywhere through `hhmm` in `src/lib/time.ts`.
- **Focus session (WI-027, ADR-021):** every Start goes to `/focus?block=<id>` (`src/app/focus/page.tsx`, client `src/features/execution/focus.tsx`, pure `focus-domain.ts`). Begin is the existing start action; the ring drains to the block's planned end (`focusTarget`); at time up an opt-in chime (`src/lib/sound.ts`, cookie `silsila-focus-chime`) and "What did you learn?". Intention and learned lines are appended to `ActualSession.notes` (`appendNote`), never replacing text; nothing completes automatically. Pop out (Document Picture-in-Picture) and Keep screen on (`src/lib/wake-lock.ts`) render only where the browser has them. The running strip and the launcher link to Focus.
- **Close the Day (WI-029):** `closeDayOpen` (`src/features/close-day/domain.ts`) opens it on a past day and on today from the stop time (`stopTime`: the `DAILY_PROGRESS` preferred time, default 21:30). `CloseDayForm` is one form with three steps; `previewSlots` and `tomorrowSlot` place each unrecorded block at the same time on the owner's tomorrow or the next free quarter hour. `closeDay` applies every choice under one lock through `saveBlockIn`, `executeIn` (skip reason "Let go", complete) and `recordReview`, and refuses the whole close when a slot is taken, a block changed, a choice is missing or a session runs. Interview blocks are never moved; recorded blocks are Done or Let go.
- **Week at a glance, motion, notes (WI-010):** per-day planned/recorded minutes in `scheduleWeek` and a bar chart on review step one; motion only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; `QuickNote` captured from the shell, listed on Today until done. No streak, no score.
- **UX simplification (WI-009):** first-run cards on Today, Practice, Learn and Jobs; visible primary actions (`Disclosure` replaces `<details>` for forms); plain vocabulary; filters and counters only once data exists; three-screen weekly review; sidebar then Today / Plan / Roadmap / Practice / Learn / Jobs / Weekly review / Settings (today: Learn by URL only, Ask Rumi last); Google Calendar panel lives in Settings; inline form feedback with a pending state and auto-clearing success.
- **Settings as rows (WI-025; split in WI-127 into `settingsPage()` in `settings/page-data.ts` and `settings/sections/`):** `src/app/settings/page.tsx` shows one record line (`settingsSummary`) and groups of `SettingRow`s (`src/features/settings/rows.tsx`): reminders open their forms in panels (`reminderContext` reads "21:30 · On"), the Google Calendar panel is a heading with Sync now plus rows and panels, Theme is `ThemeSwitch` (saves on choice), shortcuts a toggle, system status one line.

## Study

- **DSA (WI-003; the ladder since WI-094, ADR-029):** topics, problems, append-only attempts, RED/YELLOW/GREEN confidence shown as Shaky/Okay/Solid and setting no dates; a revision ladder per problem of three steps 3, 7 and 14 days from the day it was solved (`revisionStartedOn`, `revisionOutcomes` of `revised`/`missed`, `nextRevisionAt` the open step's day), each revised by Mark revised or an attempt, or missed once its day passes (`settleRevisions` runs before Practice is read and before the reminder counts); nothing is overdue. The due queue is today's open steps.
- **Technical learning (WI-004):** subjects, topics, four 0–3 mastery dimensions, append-only activities, review dates.
- **Goals and roadmap (WI-009):** `Goal` authoring with target dates and live weekly progress; `Roadmap` → `RoadmapItem` (sections, concepts/questions, status, asked-in-interview mark, optional links to Learn topics and Practice problems/topics). A roadmap is a planning layer: it never changes mastery, attempts or revision dates (ADR-016). Today shows the chain (14 days, record not score) in its Activity panel, a live timer and keyboard shortcuts; the running session sits in the shell on every page.
- **The Roadmap as the tracker (WI-012, ADR-018):** items carry scope, checkpoint, priority (P0–P2), estimate, read-only references, source and an import slug; roadmaps carry a study prompt template and scenarios; **Copy prompt** renders on the client (`src/features/roadmap/study-prompt.tsx`) so the owner studies in Claude or ChatGPT and marks the item here. Filters above eight items. Material is imported, never committed: `pnpm roadmap:bundle` (parsers `scripts/lib/roadmap-sources.ts`; researched curriculum as data in `scripts/lib/dsa-curriculum.ts` and `scripts/lib/interview-research.ts`) then `pnpm roadmap:import` (idempotent; keyed by roadmap title, item slug, topic name, problem URL; never overwrites marks, attempts, confidence or owner notes). DSA problems from the bundle are `DsaProblem`s in Practice. `scripts/reset-owner-data.sql` was the one-time production reset (replaced by `scripts/fresh-start.sql`, WI-045). Item notes, references and the bundle are owner material: never logged; in the export. **Revision lives in the roadmap:** Done schedules `nextRevisionAt` (DATE label) 7 days out, Revised moves it to 14/30/60 (`scheduleRevision`), Reopen clears it; Today's "Revise today" card and the `TECHNICAL_REVIEW` reminder read it. **Learn is off the navigation** since WI-012 (routes, data and tests kept; removal is a later decision).
- **Roadmap as weeks (WI-088; rows since WI-023):** `src/app/roadmap/page.tsx` is the goal card, one line to This week and the revision panel, the roadmaps as bands opening to their sections (`RoadmapBands`), and Add your own roadmap; `src/app/roadmap/[id]/page.tsx` is one section as its own page with its items as rows that open in place (`WeekItems`), counts as "N done · M left" (no bars, ratios or pace), revision as "Revision overdue by N days", Add items, Edit roadmap and Edit item in side panels. Links of the form `/roadmap/<id>?section=…#item-…` keep working: the section is the page, the hash the open row.
- **This week (WI-048):** `WEEKLY_MIX` (3 DSA problems, 1 code review, 1 bug squash, 1 practical, 1 system design, 2 behavioural) in `src/features/roadmap/week.ts`; `weekMix` counts distinct problems attempted this week for coding and items with `completedAt` in the owner's week for the rest (`formatSet`), takes the next item from `formatPractice`, and puts formats of interviews within `INTERVIEW_HORIZON_DAYS` (14) first. `readyLine` names the nearest active goal with a date and the P0 items left; `planQuestion` is what Plan with Rumi hands over. `formatItems` (`jobs/service.ts`) is the one query both the loop card and the week use. Counts toward a plan, never a score or pace.
- **Practice drills (WI-030):** `drillDeck` (`src/features/drills/deck.ts`) picks ten of the owner's problems (Red, Yellow, unattempted, oldest seen; ties by a day seed) with the topic and three same-family distractors from curriculum patterns or topics holding the owner's problems; the signal is `DsaTopic.description`. `src/app/dsa/drill/page.tsx` reuses `dsaSnapshot`. `trace` in `src/features/drills/trace.ts` traces two pointers, sliding window, binary search and monotonic stack for topics `traceForTopic` maps, shown on `/dsa/[id]`; `bruteForce` is the test oracle. Drills never write: no attempts, no confidence, no revision dates.
- **Revision deck (WI-032):** `revisionDeck` (kept `RevisionCard`s on items `revisionDue` returns, in due order) feeds `/roadmap/deck` (`src/features/ai/deck.tsx`); `revisionDeckRow` adds the Today row from `revisionDue`'s `_count.revisionCards`. The Practice problem hints of WI-032 were removed in WI-038 (hints will come from Rumi); `AiFeature.GRILL_PROBLEM` stays for its ledger rows. A retry never resends the rejected answer, and failures log rule codes only. AI previews show `SentFields` (`sentRows`), a readable list, never raw JSON.
- **Grill Me (WI-031, ADR-022):** `src/features/ai/`. `studyInput` is the only path from an item to a model (ticked title, scope, checkpoint, reference names; never notes, `askedNote`, links). `requestBody` pins `MODEL`/`ENDPOINT` with `only`, no fallback, `zdr`, `data_collection: "deny"`, `require_parameters` and never sends unsupported parameters (`SENT_PARAMETERS`; no `temperature`). `grill` reserves each call in `AiUsage` under the owner lock (the owner's monthly limit, `AiSetting.monthlyLimitMicros`, none by default since WI-114; 20/hour, 60/day), settles the cost, retries once on a bad ladder, and blocks AI when the answer is not from `Google`. `evaluateProvider` checks OpenRouter's public lists; a failure (daily cron or stale check) sets `AiSetting.blockedAt`, lifted only by the owner's Check again. `RevisionCard` holds kept rungs. `aiConfig()` needs `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`; without it no AI control renders. `Roadmap.studyAi` gates per roadmap.
- **Phase 4 (from WI-033):** the owner chose to build the Phase 4 hypotheses now (2026-10-26) instead of after 14 days. **Day Print (WI-033):** `dayPrint`/`layoutRings` (`src/features/day-print/domain.ts`) turn a reviewed day's sessions into rings (fixed light-token hex colours by `categoryGroup`); `DayPrintCard` on Today for reviewed days unless `User.dayPrint === false`; `SavePrint` rasterizes the SVG to PNG on the device; `dayPrintAction` stores keep/remove. **Look Back (WI-034):** `lookBack` (`src/features/look-back/service.ts`) feeds `seasonGrid`, `miniRings`, `moments` and `monthFacts` (pure, `domain.ts`); test-named records never become moments or month facts; no writes, no cache. **Ambient sound (WI-035):** `playAmbient`/`stopAmbient` (`src/lib/ambient.ts`) on the shared `audioContext()`; the choice is the `silsila-focus-sound` cookie (off by default); it starts only after a gesture (`audioRunning()`), fades out at time up, and the sounds that played are appended to `ActualSession.notes` as `Sound: …` (`soundsLine`). **Command bar (WI-036):** `CommandBar` and `CommandButton` (`src/features/command/bar.tsx`, mounted in the layout) read `GET /api/command` (`todayCommands`) on open and `POST /api/command` (`searchCommands`, words in the body) while typing; `score`/`rankCommands` (pure, `domain.ts`) need every typed word, keep a fixed group order and five rows a group; `explicit` commands (Grill me) show only when named. A command opens `href`, then a `data-panel` opener or a `press` selector once that page shows it (`afterNavigation`); Add a note calls `addNoteAction`, the bar's only write. **Listen (WI-037, ADR-023):** `listen` (`src/features/listen/service.ts`) fetches only `sourcePlan` links (HTTPS, `OFFICIAL`/`SECONDARY` lists, four at most, redirects only to listed sites), `paragraphsOf` and `pickPassages` make at most nine passages, `ask()` sends `listenMessages` (item fields and passages, never notes) as `LISTEN_ITEM`, and `crossCheck` keeps a line only when an official passage, or passages from two sites, `supports` it; fewer than four lines shows nothing. `Listen` (`listen.tsx`) reads it with Web Speech; nothing is stored.
- **Interview invite research (WI-105):** `src/features/prep/invite.ts` (`INVITE_FORMAT`, `inviteMessages`, `readInvite` with topics only from `INVITE_TOPICS`, `roundEnd`, `studySlots`); `parseInvite`, `addInviteRound`, `tickInviteFormats` in the prep service; `planFromDoc` (`src/features/prep/plan.ts`). The prep doc gains What this round asks, What to study in order, From your own material and Plan until the date.

## Jobs

- **Jobs (WI-005):** applications, stage history, next actions with action ownership, multiple InterviewRounds per application, prep items, DSA/learning links, reminders.
- **Jobs as moves (WI-022; redrawn in WI-096; the application page split in WI-126 into `applicationPage()` in `jobs/page-data.ts` and `jobs/application/`):** `src/app/jobs/page.tsx` and `src/app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx` read like Today (since WI-096 the list is two-line rows under a Practice-style head with a rail of the next interviews, and the application page leads with the stage as a pipeline row, the next action strip and the interviews): `applicationMove` and `pagePrimary` in `src/features/jobs/domain.ts` name the next move and the one primary; the Needs attention rail and Today's share `src/features/attention/domain.ts` (`jobAttentionRows`, `interviewPrepRows`, revision rows, `dueContext`); the side panel is `src/components/side-panel.tsx`. The interview's schedule block follows the round: a result settles it, a reschedule or "Add back to schedule" revives it, a rename renames a block still carrying the generated "Company — Round" title (`scheduleBlockTitle`).
- **Interview formats (WI-047):** `src/features/jobs/formats.ts` (pure) names every round type (`typeLabel`; the older ones read as `label` did, since a round's default title is its type) and maps each practice format to a guide item and drill sets by roadmap title, section and import slug (`FORMAT_PRACTICE`; P0 problem items for coding, the company's own `Company: …` behavioural section through `companyMatches`). `loopFormats` joins `JobApplication.formats` with upcoming rounds' types and a single stated `aiPolicy`; `loopPractice` (`service.ts`) loads the matching items in one query. `saveFormats` and `formatsAction` write the ticks. Rumi's `get_jobs`, the prep searches and prep messages carry formats and AI rules; nothing new reaches a search beyond fixed format words.
- **Prep docs (WI-042, ADR-024):** `src/features/prep/`. `searchQueries` (`domain.ts`) builds at most six searches from company, role and round types only; `searchBody` sends each alone with the `web` plugin (Exa, three results) on `PINNED_PROVIDER`. `writePrepDoc` (`service.ts`) runs them through `callModel` (`PREP_RESEARCH`, no per-call rate; five docs a day), keeps answering searches, numbers sources with `collectSources` (`isOfficial`: the company's name as a host label, or the posting's host), then `ask()` with `PREP_FORMAT` (3,000 tokens) and `prepMessages` (notes with their source ids, rounds, Study AI roadmap item and problem titles as R#/P#, the job description only when ticked); `readPrepDoc` drops unsourced points and unknown links and refuses a doc under three sourced points. `InterviewPrepDoc` stores the doc and its cited sources; the page is `src/app/jobs/[id]/prep/[docId]/page.tsx`, the panel `src/features/prep/panel.tsx`, and Rumi reads the latest docs with `get_prep_docs`. Grill Me's per-call limits skip `PREP_RESEARCH` rows.
- **Debrief and mock interview (WI-108):** `src/features/prep/mock-domain.ts` (`DEBRIEF_QUESTIONS`, `mockQuestions`, `hasScore`, `cleanFeedback`, `MOCK_SYSTEM`) and `mock.ts` (`debriefRound` through `recordRoundResult`, `mockFor`, `mockFeedback` as an Ask Rumi conversation); `DebriefPanel` on completed rounds, `MockInterview` at `/jobs/[id]/prep/[docId]/mock`.

## Rumi

- **Ask Rumi (WI-040, ADR-024; persona and memory since WI-100, ADR-030):** `src/features/agent/`. Since WI-100 every prompt starts from `PERSONA` plus a style line, and "Rumi knows" is kept by Rumi itself from the owner's own messages (`memory-domain.ts`, `memory-service.ts`: kinds, caps 40 / 25, expiry, dedupe, a deterministic gate before one strict call), shown under the answer with Undo and in Settings by kind. `TOOLS` and `TOOL_ARGS` (`domain.ts`) are 23 tools: ten reads (`get_today`, `get_schedule`, `get_activity`, `get_roadmaps`, `get_practice`, `get_jobs`, `get_patterns`, `get_prep_docs`, `find_problems`, `find_items`), the plan card `propose_blocks`, three small writes and nine cards (below); `runTool` (`tools.ts`) selects only the fields it sends, so the never-read list is never loaded; `titleVisible` sends titles only for the owner's study categories (Study nudges setting) plus DSA, Technical and job search, the rest as "busy"; Study AI off roadmaps and test-named records are left out. `sendMessage` (`service.ts`) counts and stores the owner's message under the lock (`AGENT_RATE` 30/hour, 100/day), sends `systemPrompt` (date, rules, `BANNED_WORDS`, "Rumi knows" lines) plus `historyWindow` (12 messages), and loops up to `MAX_ROUNDS` = 6 `callModel` calls (`chatBody`: the `PINNED_PROVIDER` routing, tools, free text, `tool_choice: "none"` on the last); only the question and the answer are stored, with tool names and cost in `metadata`. `callModel` in `src/features/ai/service.ts` is now the one reserve-post-check path for `ask()` and Rumi; Grill Me's per-call limits skip `AGENT_CHAT` rows. `evaluateProvider` also requires `CHAT_PARAMETERS` (`tools`, `tool_choice`). `RumiText` renders `renderText` blocks as text only. The command bar hands "Ask Rumi · …" over in sessionStorage (`src/features/agent/events.ts`). User-facing name is `AGENT_NAME`; code says `agent`.
- **Rumi plans (WI-041):** `propose_blocks` (`src/features/agent/domain.ts`) makes cards, never writes (`suggest_memory` left `TOOLS` in WI-100; its old cards still render): the message loop calls `proposeBlocks` (`plans.ts`), which refuses days outside `planAhead` (today and six more since WI-116; `planWindow`, today to this Sunday, before), runs `generateThroughSunday`, counts the routines of a day in an unprepared week (`routinesOn`), and marks each block with `checkBlocks` (`plan-domain.ts`: passed, inverted, overlapping the day or an earlier block; conflicts named via `takenLabel`, "busy" outside study). Cards live in `AgentMessage.metadata` (`proposals`, `suggestions`, read by `readMetadata`). `applyProposal` loads the answer by id and owner, generates that day (the whole week for a card with `prepares`, ADR-031), re-checks under the lock and writes every block through `saveBlockIn` or none; undo `cancel-blocks` cancels them and `reopenProposal` shows the card again. `answerSuggestion` keeps a line (duplicates skipped, 20 at most). `getPatterns` (`tools.ts`) feeds `patterns`/`patternLine` (best three-hour window, recorded share of planned per kind, 28 days) for Rumi and Weekly review's `.review-patterns` line.
- **Rumi's small writes (WI-102, ADR-030 class A):** `add_note`, `add_block` (one block in free time today to Sunday, three hours at most) and `rest_today` run at once inside the answer, under the owner lock, through the pages' writers (`src/features/agent/writes.ts`), two an answer and twenty a day, never in the last two rounds; each is stored on the answer (`metadata.actions`) with a persistent Undo (`ActionRows`, `undoWriteAction`, only the undo kind its tool leaves). Reads carry ids (visible blocks, applications, rounds, problems; `find_problems`) and an answer keeps them as `metadata.seen`; a write that names a record must have read it. Everything else stays a card: since WI-103, `move_block`, `delete_block`, `change_stage`, `log_attempt` (no Undo) and `write_review` (a draft the owner edits), and since WI-118 `mark_item`, `add_application`, `add_round` and `set_goal`, are cards (`metadata.requests`) applied by the owner's press in one owner lock against the card's snapshot (`src/features/agent/requests.ts`), with Undo stored on the answer.
- **Rumi noticed (WI-043; the pattern coach since WI-106):** `noticed` (`src/features/noticed/domain.ts`, pure, no AI) makes at most `NOTICED_PER_DAY` = 2 cards in this order: an interview 1–3 days out with open prep, a stuck topic (four of seven days, confidence not risen) or a roadmap item in progress four days, a revision due on a topic not practised four days, a study kind silent for `IDLE_DAYS` (5) that was planned or recorded in 28 days, a routine left on every occurrence for three weeks, and today or tomorrow planned at twice the usual recorded study (and 90 minutes more). `noticedFor` (`service.ts`, memoized) reads only study kinds (`isStudyCategory` with the owner's Study nudges categories plus DSA and Technical) and skips test names; `NoticedRows` (`rows.tsx`) sits at the top of Today's Needs attention; an answer (`answerNoticedAction`) upserts `NoticedAnswer` (`actedAt` or `dismissedAt`, `hiddenUntil` +3 or +7 days). Actions hand words to Ask Rumi through `ASK_KEY`, or open a page, and Open routines opens the Plan's routines panel through `openPanelAfterNavigation` (`src/features/command/bar.tsx`). Templates are checked against `BANNED_WORDS` (`cleanCopy`).
- **Rumi's cards (WI-103, WI-118, ADR-030 class B):** `move_block`, `delete_block`, `change_stage`, `log_attempt` (no Undo, attempts are append-only), `write_review` (a draft the owner edits), `mark_item`, `add_application`, `add_round` and `set_goal` become cards (`request-domain.ts`: `requestArgs`, `REQUEST_ID_ARGS`, `REQUEST_UNDO`, `readRequests`) applied by the owner's press in one owner lock against the card's snapshot (`requests.ts`: `prepareRequest`, `applyRequest`, `dismissRequest`, `undoRequest`).
- **Plans ahead (WI-116, ADR-031):** `planAhead` (today and six more days) and `preparesWeek` in `plan-domain.ts`; a card for a day in an unprepared week counts that week's routines (`routinesOn`) and its press prepares the week first ("Prepare the week and add N blocks").
- **Page context (WI-117):** `pageRef`, `pageInput`, `promptsFor`, `isSunday` (`page-domain.ts`) and `readPage` (`page-context.ts`): the drawer sends its path, the named record is read for the owner only without private fields, and its ids join `seen`. Questions by page; Sunday evening offers "Plan next week with Rumi".
- **Streaming (WI-119):** `callModel(…, onStream)` and the pure `streamReader` (`src/features/ai/stream.ts`); `POST /api/rumi/ask` sends `delta` and `reset` lines and the chat shows a draft until the stored answer.
- **Voice (WI-104, ADR-030):** Settings → Voice (consent in `AgentSetting.voiceConsentAt`, language, `speakReplies`, Check this device); `src/lib/speech.ts` (`useDictation`, `useSpeaker`, `useVoices`); the microphone in the composer and Listen on Today's line card (`src/features/agent/voice.tsx`, `voice-domain.ts`). A voice message is stored as text with only `{ via, edited }` beside it; no audio is kept.
- **Rumi's character (WI-111 to WI-113, WI-115):** the owner's renders as webp poses in `public/mascot/` (`POSES`, `MASCOT_VERSION` in `mascot.ts`; `RumiPose` and `Lids` in `pose.tsx`; `RumiMark` in `mark.tsx`) on a cream tile, one pose per place. The round button (`rumi-button.tsx`) can be hidden or moved to the other corner (`silsila-rumi-button` cookie, `button-pref.ts`) and folds small while a pose shows; ⌘/ or Ctrl+/ toggles the drawer.
- **The AI limit (WI-114):** `AiSetting.monthlyLimitMicros` is the owner's monthly limit, null for none (US$0.10 to US$1000, checked by the database); `withinLimit`, `nearLimit`, `underShare`, `budgetLine`, `LIMIT_CHOICES` in `src/features/ai/domain.ts`; Settings → AI and Grill Me. Never hard-code a dollar cap.

## Notifications

- **Study Nudges (WI-028):** `StudyNudgeSetting` per owner (both modes off by default). Pure rules `morningDue` and `eveningDue` in `src/features/nudges/domain.ts`; `processStudyNudges` runs first in the notification job and writes `STUDY_MORNING` / `STUDY_EVENING` rows keyed once per local day (`nudgeKey`). Rows are `inAppOnly` while `User.lastActiveAt` (the shell's `PresenceBeat`, `POST /api/presence`) is under five minutes old, and push skips them. The evening check-in keeps `DAILY_PROGRESS` in the app and suppresses `OVERDUE_TASK` for study blocks. Today's `NudgeCard` (`#study-nudge`) confirms every write: Move to tomorrow (`saveBlock`, closes the nudge), Rest today (`DailyPlan.restAt`, undo `clear-rest-day`), feedback. Copy is fixed and reviewed (`MORNING_LINES`, `EVENING_LINES`, `BANNED_WORDS`); never "missed" or "you didn't". WI-039 adds `blockStartDue` (type `STUDY_BLOCK`, `StudyNudgeSetting.blockStartEnabled`/`blockStartMinutes`, one a day, upcoming and overdue reminders skip study blocks while it is on), `letGoAction`, and fact templates `morningBody`/`eveningBody`/`blockBody`. WI-101 (ADR-030) lets Rumi write the three lines: with `rumiLines` on, the morning, afternoon (`STUDY_MIDDAY`, `middayDue`) and evening (`recapDue`, every active evening) rows are queued with their template and `metadata.rumi = "pending"`; `writeRumiLines` (`src/features/agent/lines.ts`, outside every lock, one row per cron run) runs fixed reads, one strict `AGENT_LINE` call and `lineOk` (`line-domain.ts`: 220 characters, plain, no banned word, every number from the reads), keeps the template on failure, and push waits at most two minutes; "Too many" rests the afternoon line a week.
- **Readiness R1–R3 (Version 2):** `isTestNamed` (`src/lib/test-data.ts`) marks test/UAT-named records; `pnpm data:check` lists them read-only.
