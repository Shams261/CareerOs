# WI-084 handoff — the roadmap as an outline and a reading pane

Date: 2026-10-30. Branch `feat/wi-084-roadmap-pane` from `main` `c91050b` (WI-083).

**Source.** The owner found a roadmap's page cluttered: a column of section names, an accordion of items on the right, the open item with Study / Notes tabs, a boxed Done when, a scenario select and a doubt field, several items open at once. They asked for a minimal, modern layout where opening one item closes the others, and for the choice to rest on research rather than taste. Two passes were made: published guidance (NN/g on accordions, modals, tabs and progressive disclosure; the Android list-detail layout; Notion's side peek), then a field survey on 2026-10-30 of twenty learning-path, interview-prep, docs and task apps at 1440 px and 390 px (The Odin Project, NeetCode Courses and Practice, MDN Learn, Khan Academy, CS50, Linear and Stripe docs, Notion help, LeetCode study plans, Grind 75, roadmap.sh, Exercism, freeCodeCamp, Coursera and the sales pages of Codecademy, educative, Frontend Masters and Brilliant). Thirteen of twenty show the outline beside the open item; none study inside an accordion; one opens a drawer (roadmap.sh, because its canvas is a diagram); none put tabs inside the item. The owner chose the outline + reading pane with the status as one segmented control.

**Migration:** none.
