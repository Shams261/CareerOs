# WI-020 handoff — Today as an agenda

Branch `feat/wi-020-today-agenda` from `main` at `be59255` (WI-019 merged, PR #25). Scope: the owner's specification "WI-020 — Today: Agenda-first redesign" with three decisions confirmed on 2026-10-22: no progress ring (one factual summary line instead), the daily review in a side panel, and the WI-017 global tokens kept (no Today-only palette). No schema change, no migration, no new dependency, no route change. Not pushed.

## 1. Outcome

- **Header.** "Today" (or "Past day" / "Upcoming day") with the date beside it (20 px, regular weight) and quiet day navigation on the same line, and the utilities Activity ↗ · Notes (with the open count) · Review day (or Edit review once saved); under 1100 px they sit behind a labelled More. One factual line: "1 block completed · 59 min recorded · 2h 15m planned" (all categories, cancelled blocks excluded, clipped to the local day, a running session counted to now, sessions counted once). "Add a block" sits in the "Your day" heading as a link, with its `N` shortcut and its draft key.
- **Launcher.** One compact surface, the primary action on the right with the live timer stacked above it while a session runs, a divider and the "Next: …" line under the context, and a fixed child order: a running session ("In session · Since 15:24", the live timer, "Stop session"), else the eligible block covering now ("Now · 10:00–10:30", "Start session", "Open prep" for an interview, "Next: …"), else the next eligible block ("Starts in 40 min", Start session allowed early), else "Update earlier blocks" when past blocks still need a decision, else a quiet "Nothing more planned" with Add a block. Secondary actions sit behind More. Running identity comes from the session record, so a reviewed day with a running session still shows the running controls.
- **Agenda ("Your day").** An open list with a time gutter and a timeline rail with a dot per row (filled when done, teal when current, hollow later), grouped Earlier / Now / Later on today only (non-empty groups, each label with a rule, the Now rule in teal), the current block on a light teal wash with a narrow accent rule, completed rows quieter; each row shows the start time, the title with a neutral category icon (a block the Jobs round form named "Company — Round" shows the round's title, since the company is on the meta line; `blockTitle`), one meta line ("45 min recorded", "No session recorded", "Two pointers · 60 min", "Example Labs · Until 10:30"), a status with an icon on the right (a check for Done, whose Reset sits behind ···; an amber dot for Not recorded, a teal dot for In session) and a chevron that opens the details panel; "View full schedule →" closes the list. A past block without a decision reads "Not recorded" (no session) or "Recorded · Needs update" (sessions exist) and offers Log time, Reschedule and More; skipped and cancelled blocks are never "missed". More exposes Complete, Skip and Cancel block with labels; Skip asks for its reason only after Skip is chosen. Cancelled blocks sit in a collapsed "Cancelled · N" disclosure with Reset and Details. Each row has a Details button; the row the launcher already controls has no second Start/Stop.
- **Interviews.** A round linked to a block through `TimeBlock.interviewRoundId` is one row (the block's controls plus the interview context and Open prep); rounds without a block are their own rows and show their start time only when they have no end; nothing is deduplicated by title or time, and nothing is created from the page.
- **Needs attention.** A flat list in the right rail, each row an icon tile coloured by source as in the owner's reference (teal for Jobs and interview prep, lavender for Practice, blue for the roadmap, amber for a reminder, green for the weekly review), the source as its label (Jobs, Practice, Roadmap, Reminder, Weekly review), a title that names the move ("Send follow-up", "Review Two Sum", "Revisit HTTP semantics", "Prepare System design", "Log the Coding result"), the context ("Example Labs · Due today", "Revision due today · Yellow", "Example Labs · Tomorrow at 14:00", a reminder's "Today" or its weekday date) and a chevron; five rows after deduplication, "Showing 5 of N", "View all N" opening a panel paged by twenty. Sources: interview prep today, job follow-ups and results needing logging, Practice revisions, roadmap revisions, unread due reminders, the Sunday weekly-review prompt. Notes and goals are not attention. Tiers: prep for an interview today → overdue follow-ups and results → due today (revisions, reminders, follow-ups) → upcoming; within a tier by due instant then stable id. A reminder merges into the row of the entity its `dedupeKey` names (INTERVIEW → round, JOB_FOLLOW_UP → application) and keeps its ids, so Mark read clears the reminder while the task stays; a reminder keyed on one of today's blocks (UPCOMING_BLOCK, OVERDUE_TASK) reads as that block ("Cover letter draft · Not recorded · 11:00–12:00", "Starts at 18:00") and links to its row; other unmatched reminders stay as rows; Practice and roadmap revisions never merge. Each source loads with `Promise.allSettled`: a failed source is named while the rest show; the whole list sits behind its own Suspense and error boundary ("Couldn’t load attention items. Retry"), so the agenda never waits for it. Empty: "Nothing needs your attention."
- **Panels.** `src/features/today/panels.tsx`: one modal side panel at a time (440 px on the right, a full sheet under 700 px), rendered through a portal at the end of `<body>` so `inert` covers the shell, the navigation and the running strip; focus moves to the heading, Tab cycles inside, Escape and the Close button close, focus returns to the opener, page scroll is locked, the URL and the agenda scroll are untouched, and a navigation closes it. Children render only while open, so one block form is mounted at a time. Panels: block details (recorded sessions, Log time, Edit this day), Review day / Edit review, Activity (the 14-day chain with its day links), Notes (the existing list with Done, Remove and Undo), Needs attention (all rows, paged).
- **Daily review.** Opened only from the header. The compact form keeps the action, the field names, validation and the per-day draft: the rating is five segments plus "No rating" (the clear), the reflection is one field, blocker and carry-to-tomorrow sit under More. Saving shows "Daily review saved." in the header, marks the summary line Reviewed, closes the panel, returns focus and clears the draft only after the server answered; a refusal keeps the panel open with the message. Future dates get no review button.
- **Shared changes, opt-in.** `ExecutionControls` gains `launcher`, `hideStart` and `more` (defaults unchanged); `SecondaryActions` gains `always` and the two-step `SkipStep`; `DisclosureContext` is exported so a panel can offer `close()` to forms; `ExecutionRefresh` and `PageShortcuts` stay quiet while `data-panel-open` is set (interval and window-focus refresh, single-key shortcuts); `readNotificationAction` is the `ActionForm` twin of `readNotification` (several ids, one confirmation); `weeklyReviewOpen` lifts the Sunday rule into the review service.
- **Removed from Today.** The stats row, the running hero, the Goals, Revise today, Jobs, Technical prep, DSA, Reminders and Notes cards and the daily-review card. The Today-only components (`TodayGoals`, `TodayRevision`, `TodayJobs`, `TodayInterview`, `TodayLearning`, `TodayDsa`, `WeeklyReviewPrompt`) are deleted; every service and domain helper behind them stays (Learn, Practice, Jobs and the Roadmap pages still use them), as do `InterviewMentions` and `Chain`.

## 4. Known limitations and differences from the reference

- Not exercised in the browser: a twelve-block day and a queue of twenty-plus attention items (the dev database has neither; ordering, deduplication and the twenty-row pages are covered by the domain tests), 200 % zoom and reduced motion (CSS only: the panel slide and the row and launcher transitions are inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`), a screen reader. Browser checks ran at 1000 px rather than exactly 1100 and 768; the breakpoints are 1099 and 700.
- Against the reference image: the brand mark is the existing component and the day navigation stays beside the date (quiet chevrons and a date field). Times on Today read as 24-hour ("06:00", "19:30") through `hhmm` in the Today domain, as the reference does; the rest of the app keeps its 12-hour format. The tiles are coloured by source and the header carries "+ Add" and "Review day" as the reference shows.
- The palette is the global WI-017 token set, as the owner decided; the only Today-scoped value is `--row-tint` (`#EDF3EF`, `#1B2E29` in dark).
- "Starts in 40 min" and the Earlier / Now / Later split are rendered on the server and move with the five-minute refresh and the focus refresh (both pause while a panel is open); the timer is the only client clock.
- Marking a merged reminder read keeps the underlying task row; the next refresh of the rail backfills the preview from the full queue.
- Dev database side effects of the checks (nothing in production): a "WI-020 earlier block" (cancelled), a "Reading" 07:00 block left unresolved, one short DSA session, the daily review for 2026-10-22 saved with the notes "WI-020 review check" and no rating, one reminder marked read.
