# WI-012 handoff — the Roadmap is the tracker

Branch `feat/wi-012-roadmap-tracker` on `main` after PR #17. Plan agreed 2026-10-21 after the owner's clarification: the Roadmap must do what the two HTML "command center" trackers did (scope, checkpoint, priority, references, copy-ready study prompts per topic), for every stream, inside Silsila. The AI preparation-suggestions part of the plan was dropped by the owner before implementation.

## 1. Outcome

- **Roadmap items are trackable topics.** `RoadmapItem` gains `scope`, `checkpoint`, `priority` (1–3, shown as P0–P2), `estimateMinutes`, `references` (read-only curated links), `sourceRef` and `slug` (unique per roadmap, the import key); `notes` is the owner's revision space with a 20 000-character limit. `Roadmap` gains `promptTemplate` and `scenarios`. Migration `20261021204620_roadmap_tracker_fields` (additive; defaults keep every existing row valid).
- **Study panel and Copy prompt** on every item: scope, done-means, references, notes, source; a scenario select, an optional doubt or link, and a Copy button that renders the roadmap's template on the client (`renderPrompt` in `src/features/roadmap/domain.ts`) and writes it to the clipboard, with a selectable textarea when the clipboard is unavailable. Built-in template and six scenarios when a roadmap has none; both editable on the roadmap form.
- **Tracker navigation.** Status and priority filters above eight items; Continue on the roadmap and on the overview (first item in progress, else first not started, in section order); sections with done/total on the overview; Next per goal on Today.
- **Import pipeline, nothing personal committed.** `pnpm roadmap:bundle "<folder>"` parses the backend tracker (8 weeks + beyond, 59 topics with their curated references, 12 scenarios, the starter prompt normalised to `{{…}}` placeholders), the Hello Interview tracker (12 units, 41 lessons, references, 11 scenarios), the technical roadmap (areas A–E, 19 topics with scope and checkpoint; guidance sections as items), the DSA pattern book (20 chapters via `pdftotext`: titles from the contents, page ranges, intro, recognition signals, template headings) and the behavioural handbook (13 stories with their 30 s / 90 s answers, follow-ups, deep dive and risks as notes; narrative; selection matrix; 24-question bank; cross-examination; architecture cards; rubric and protocols; the four reusable prompts as scenarios; verification worksheet; addendum of answered follow-ups). `pnpm roadmap:import <bundle>` upserts everything for the single owner; re-runs never touch status, completion, interview marks, attempts, confidence or owner notes.
- **Researched material, as data with sources** (`scripts/lib/dsa-curriculum.ts`, `scripts/lib/interview-research.ts`): the Technical mastery roadmap opens with twelve 2025–2026 interview formats (online assessment, coding screen, LeetCode-style onsite, practical coding and pairing, bug squash, integration/API, code review, take-home, mid-level system design, AI-assisted versus AI-banned, project deep dive, behavioural) plus per-company loops; the DSA roadmap is sixteen patterns in NeetCode dependency order with the core problems per pattern (NeetCode 150 / Blind 75 / Grind 75 / Prashad cross-checked, 160 core, the rest of the Prashad list as "More practice" at P2), design-a-class, eleven practical tasks product companies ask (rate limiter, CSV validation and cycle detection, transaction balances, currency graph, log redaction and mini IAM, route matching, backoff and idempotency, product ordering, grid rover, access logs, money in cents), six review and bug-squash drills with `review` and `bugsquash` prompt scenarios, and the JavaScript toolkit; the Behavioural roadmap gets per-company frameworks (Amazon's principles, Stripe's six current operating principles, Shopify Life Story, Wealthsimple values and Bring Your Own System, Google, Meta, Microsoft, YC startups, Canadian scale-ups) each with questions, a 76-question bank grouped by competency with the companies that ask it and the handbook's best/backup story mapping, ten project deep-dive probes and ten delivery rules. Every research item carries its sources, marked official, prep site or anecdotal.
- **Production reset** for the owner's one-time clean-out: `scripts/reset-owner-data.sql` (one transaction, refuses unless exactly one `User`, keeps the owner, sessions, devices, preferences, calendar connection, job runs and migrations) with its test and runbook.
- Docs: ADR-018, import runbook (operations), reset runbook (backup-restore), onboarding section, CHANGELOG, CLAUDE.md.

- **Revision inside the roadmap (added after the owner's review):** Done schedules a revision in 7 days (`nextRevisionAt`, a DATE label), **Revised** moves it to 14, 30 and then 60 days (`scheduleRevision`), Reopen clears it, and the item form can set or clear the date. Today shows a "Revise today" card; each roadmap counts items due and filters on them; the existing `TECHNICAL_REVIEW` reminder counts roadmap items together with Learn topics and is set from Settings; push opens Today. Migration `20261021213321_roadmap_revision`. **Learn is off the navigation** (routes, data and tests kept; roadmap items may still link to Learn topics); removing its code is a later decision. Per-section "Add to this section" links are gone and "Edit roadmap" moved into the status line; the local sample roadmap from the WI-009 UI checks was deleted from the dev database.

## 6. Known limits

- The pattern book and the handbook are parsed from `pdftotext -layout` output with documented heuristics; chapter headings that wrap, the selection matrix and the addendum headings were checked by hand on this copy. A changed PDF may need a parser tweak; the tests pin the current shapes.
- Research items are dated October 2026 and partly anecdotal; each carries its sources and the uncertainty notes.
- The overview lists every section of every roadmap; with 31 behavioural sections it is long but scannable. Revisit after a week of use.
- Copy prompt needs a user gesture for the clipboard; the fallback textarea covers the rest.
