# WI-022 — Jobs: the pipeline as moves

Date: 2026-10-23. Branch `feat/wi-022-jobs-moves` from `main` `8fa9039` (WI-021). Second of the four work items that make Plan, Jobs, Roadmap and the Weekly review read like Today (ADR-019, ADR-020).

## Outcome

- **The list (`/jobs`)** reads like Today's rail. One header line ("Jobs", "Reminder settings"), one factual line ("4 active · 2 upcoming interviews · 1 your move"), then a two-column workspace: on the left "Applications" with "Add application" (inline, keeps its draft and the link to the new application), the view links, the filter form (Search, Focus, Source; the overlapping Show and Stage selects are gone, J-9) and the applications grouped by stage, each row `briefcase tile | company | the next move | role · due context | chevron` ("Send follow-up · Platform Engineer · Due today", "Log the Coding result · Interview ended Fri, Oct 23", "Prepare System design · Tomorrow at 10:00", "Wait for the company · Last activity 3 days ago", "Decide the next step · No date set", "Closed · Rejected"); the whole row is the link and names the application for assistive tech (J-11); rows sort by the move's due instant, then company (J-8); the stage pill shows only when rows are not grouped (J-12). On the right, "Needs attention": the same rows Today shows for Jobs (prep for today's interviews, then every reason of every application), five in the rail, "View all N" in a panel paged by twenty. The old attention card with joined sentences and the "Upcoming interviews" sub-list ("0 prep open") are gone (J-6, J-13). "Next job search block" and "This week" sit under the rail as one line each.
- **The application (`/jobs/[id]`)** has one filled primary per state (A-3): "Log the result" on the earliest interview whose result is due, else "Mark done" on a dated next action that is yours, else "Set next action"; everything else is a link. The company is a plain line above the role (A-10). **Next move** is one row (label, the action, "Overdue by 1 day · Your move") with Mark done, "Done with a note" and "Edit" opening the next-action panel at the right section (the "What comes next" explainer is gone, A-17). **Interviews** are rows: date tile, title with its status pill ("Result needed" in amber), one context line (type · time · interviewers · location · "On your schedule Thu, Oct 22" as a link to the block, "Schedule cancelled", or "Not on your schedule"), prep and weak-area checkboxes inline for the day of, then the moves as links (Log the result, Add prep, Reschedule, Add to schedule / Add back to schedule) and a chevron; every form of a round (result or reflection, prep item, weak area, reschedule, details) lives in one panel per round, opened at the section the link names. The five `DisclosureTabs` per round and the open-by-default result form are gone. "Add interview" stays inline with its draft. Contacts show only when filled, else "Add contacts" opens the application panel at Contacts (A-18); "Edit application" opens the same panel, grouped Basics · Contacts · Notes, with a secondary Save (A-8). Panel forms close themselves after a successful save.
- **Three schedule-block bugs fixed** in `src/features/jobs/service.ts`: a result of Cancelled or No-show cancels the linked block, Completed completes a still-planned block (`settleScheduleBlock`); rescheduling a round whose block was cancelled or skipped brings the block back on the new time, and "Add back to schedule" revives it in place instead of returning it cancelled (`moveScheduleBlock`, `addInterviewToSchedule`); renaming a round or the company renames a block still carrying the service's "Company — Round" title and never one the owner wrote (`renameScheduleBlock`). Covered by one database test with six assertions.

Today's `attentionItems` composes them and adds the reminder merge; `tests/today-domain.test.ts` runs unchanged and `tests/attention-domain.test.ts` pins the builders.
- **`src/components/side-panel.tsx`** is the side-panel primitive (moved from `src/features/today/panels.tsx`, which re-exports it). Today, Plan and Jobs use it.
- **`src/features/jobs/domain.ts`** gains `applicationMove` (the row's title, context, due and urgency), `pagePrimary` and `scheduleBlockTitle`; `nextLine` and `attentionText` are gone with the old rows.
- `NextActionBox` is now only the Mark done button (optionally the page primary); `ScheduleInterviewForm` takes a label.
